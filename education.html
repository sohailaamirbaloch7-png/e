Dental Health Theory

Understand how your teeth work and how to look after them.

1. Parts of a Tooth

Enamel is the hard outer layer that protects the tooth. Dentin lies under it and is softer. Pulp is the center with nerves and blood vessels. Once enamel is damaged, the body cannot grow it back.

2. How Cavities Form

Plaque is a sticky layer of bacteria on teeth. When we eat sugar, bacteria turn it into acid. This acid slowly dissolves enamel and creates a hole called a cavity.

3. Gum Disease

If plaque stays on teeth, it hardens into tartar and irritates gums. Early stage is gingivitis (red, bleeding gums). If ignored, it becomes periodontitis and can loosen teeth.

4. Correct Brushing

Brush two times a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste. Hold the brush at a slight angle to the gums and use gentle circles. Change your toothbrush every 3 months.

5. Food and Habits

Limit sweets, soft drinks and sticky snacks. Drink more water. Floss once a day to clean between teeth, where the brush cannot reach. Avoid smoking and chewing tobacco.

6. Regular Check-ups

Visit the dentist every 6 months, even without pain. Many problems start silently and are easy to treat if found early.
